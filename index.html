The Best Movies According to Samuel

My top 3 movies of all-time.

The woman king

The Woman King is a 2022 American historical epic film directed by Gina Prince-Bythewood. The film stars Viola Davis, Thuso Mbedu, and John Boyega, and is inspired by true events that took place in The Kingdom of Dahomey, one of the most powerful states of Africa in the 18th and 19th centuries. The film follows General Nanisca (Davis) and her daughter Nawi (Mbedu) as they train the next generation of warriors to fight against an enemy determined to destroy their way of life.

Everything Everywhere All At Once

Everything Everywhere All At Once is a 2022 American absurdist action-science fiction comedy-drama film written and directed by Daniel Kwan and Daniel Scheinert. The film stars Michelle Yeoh as a Chinese-American woman who, while being audited by the IRS, discovers that she must connect with parallel universe versions of herself to prevent a powerful being from destroying the multiverse. The film also features Stephanie Hsu, Ke Huy Quan, James Hong, and Jamie Lee Curtis in supporting roles.
